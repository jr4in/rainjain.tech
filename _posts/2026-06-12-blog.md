<time datetime="2026-06-12">Friday, 12 Jun 2026</time>

Last year was a great year, I had some great friendships and had good work but had this feeling where something seems missing. Everything seems to have collapsed at the start of this year and guess the good train does not continue. I always thought I was not good enough to love someone but now remains a "What If?" a significantly lost part of my life just because I was holding off on it saying I will enjoy things only after I do this...

I read a lot of books, comics, articles and videos on life like [3 days of happiness](https://mangak.io/three-days-of-happiness), [Energise your mind](https://www.planetayurveda.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/Energize-Your-Mind-by-Gaur-Gopal-Das.pdf), [11 Rules of Life](https://www.chetanbhagat.com/books/11-rules-for-life/), [Psychology of Money](https://lib.sairam.edu.in/wp-content/uploads/sites/18/2024/11/the-psychology-of-money-by-morgan-housel.pdf), [How to do great work](https://www.paulgraham.com/greatwork.html) and [Follow your tools](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=f_Rqf-vxBM8&t=1s). All these materials coincide to a similar bullet path and that is:

- Focus on your health you can't chase everything if your health is not good. Just eat good, have a good sleep be a bit active. You don't need a big hard framework on being healthy.
- Luck follows the curious mind stay curious and open to everything that people ask. You will always get something out of it. You are essentially making yourself a really big target to hit when you are more curious to life.
- You don't need to have a dream/path to follow. Follow whatever you are interested in and work on your toolkit for expansion. You will keep doing wonderful things. Just train your mind to do hard things in to work on your toolkit.
- Dopamine is an easy killer of your toolkit. The dopamine release by your lizard part of your brain that controls your addiction to alcohol, food or anything. So take it in less quantities as possible. Also the same dopamine is release but goes to a different part of your brain when you study/work on your toolkit. One dopamine which I consider as an exception is that is extremely rewarding for your brain is relationships you maintain with people. Not the romantic love that everyone talks about look up [Relationship Anarchy](https://theanarchistlibrary.org/category/topic/relationship-anarchy)(tldr [video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vaKeCPgay9I&t=22s&pp=ygUUcmVsYXRpb25zaGlwIGFuYXJjaHk%3D)). Romance is not the only thing you want in life you want friendships too hold on to.
- All the things which are considered important(success, money and more...) 80% of it is very easy to accomplish you might only have to work on 20% to achieve that. More if you want to achieve is your choice. But even if you keep pushing yourself there is no harm no foul.
- Spend some time to analyse yourself cause of the efforts that you apply there is a [square root n rule](https://priyanshuvishnoi.substack.com/p/the-square-root-rule-for-better-living) i.e. of everything you do 50% of the results come by the 10% of effort you put in. What you would want to do is find that 10% and expand paths beyond it.
- The time can be a ruthless beast keep expanding your things and people you meet and time will compound enough to help you out. Stay stuck and keep complaining and time compounds negatively too. It is always too late to start something but the best time is always now to do it.

With these things in my mind I realise I still am interested in computers and how technology has transformed our world and I would like to contribute my thing to this technology to help someone. To do this I will try to follow as much as possible on the top points and will post an analysis of things that I understood and prjects that I improved and important things I spent my time on... This is where the blog comes in to be a record of myself holding accountable to doing great things!!

To the people who are reading this Thank you for being here.
