Life like a game of chess is probably solved. We just might not know the solution to it yet.

Imagine I gave you a copy of GTA X an ultra realistic copy of the game but you have to return it to me tommorrow. Naturally what you would want to do is play the game whole night as much as possible try to roam around as much of the inner game world maybe up your stats and keep playing. Your life is similar... you have limited time here and it is a game where only you score yourself if you are winning or losing. A lot of stats are around to buff yourself on some [examples](http://youtube.com/watch?v=OUlO7zCpVQI) here.

This also means that other people choose to sometimes drive your game aka choose to decide on how to live your life. A lot of people get paid to spend hours in office of their time and eventually their expertise for the company. It might be required in this economy to do so too to help live someone else's dreams come true but do ask yourself what are the things that you value right now. What do you want to have done to live a more fruitfull life aka get maximum score on your life?

Stop comparing yourself with other's for the things that they want to score higher are different from what you would want. Compare yourself from the day before or months before and ask yourself are you improving. That is all that matters...

Cheat codes to life? Media and other books ofcourse. You get a deep understanding of a person's lifestyle and views of the world without living another life. Quote by George R R Martin: “A reader lives a thousand lives before he dies...”
