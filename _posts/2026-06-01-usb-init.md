Welcome to my first post, where I explain [USB 2.0](https://www.usb.org/document-library/usb-20-specification) as fast as possible. USB is a PC to device protocol where you would connect to that device with least amount of reconfiguration (Plug and Play) which aims to be at the lowest cost for wider adoption.
