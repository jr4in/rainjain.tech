## Here are some of the books that I read and what I think of them

### Three Days of Happiness
A really sad and maybe depressing story but which questions you on the what you think is the value of your life. We hold out a lot on ourselves with enjoying life. But death and the countdown of life means we never know what could happen. As a person in early twenties going into mid 20s you have similar questions as Kusunoki the protagonist on what does it mean to live a good life.

### Energise your mind
A really good way to strengthen you mental health and put barriers in place for not ruining it and ways to prevent yourself from further harm and damage.

### Your Name
A really beautiful love story between 2 people seperated by time who still don't know their name and how an event could change their life. The director and author of this Makoto Shinkai has a lot of beautiful movies. Solid reccommend.

### The courage to be disliked
A teaching on Alderian Psychology of life. A proof that life is really simple and all the issues in life is just inter-relationship problems and basic human instinct on endless chase of superiority. The Greek's Philosophical understanding on true way of living.

### 11 Rules of Life
The 11 points on living a worthful life by Chetan Bhagat. How the society lives. What is the ultimate blocker for us to not scale. 
 - Never Ignore your fitness
 - Master your emotions
 - Put yourself first
 - Master simple english
 - No cheap dopamine
 - Eat the elephant
 - Connect with people
 - Own your own mistakes
 - Earn Some & Invest

### Psychology of Money
The understanding on what is the true value of money. Why do you want to invest and work. The things which you would be sacrificing for money and the obvious is listed in the book. For the framework and guide to invest on money I would reccommmend the below one.

### Let's Talk Money: You've Worked Hard for It, Now Make It Work for You
Monika Halan has given a beautiful perspective on setting up a framework for investing and earning money for the average people out there. The basic understanding on goal setting and what each means is explained with relatable stories and anecdotes well worth the read for beginner investing.

### Surrounded by Idiots
A brief understanding on people and how they react in their default state divided into four colours.
 - Red - People who are driven to tasks and competitiveness and are generally less considerate of feelings. Highly driven
 - Yellow - The creative people who try to take up the conversations generally tend to inspire more and less focused on actually getting work done.
 - Blue - The people who take on analysis of the facts. Driven by knowledge and would rather take things slowly in pursuit of perfection.
 - Green - The majority of the friendly people who are scared of changes and generally overwhelmed. These people tend to be good to almost all of the colours without any issues.

### One Minute Teacher
A teaching on giving yourself a minute for each of the three things.
- One Minute for Realising goals of improvement and what you want to do
- One Minute Praising for doing something right
- One Minute on Recovery when you are doing something wrong

### Who moved My Cheese
A lesson on to become more accepting on life on the changes that happen and move along cause it is more beautiful to live with accepting changes that way.

### Chip War - The Fight for the World's Most Critical Technology
The incredible improvements in technology and an understanding how countries and companies control the most important era of compute and how disastrous this balance of power is. A fascinating history of Moor's Law and scale of tech.

### But How do it know
The basic principles of a processor and how it works with really good explanations for the beginners trying to understand their computers. 

### Operating Systems: Three Easy pieces
How operating systems have been designed and even further in depth on How they solve the multiprogramming paradigm of compute we expect today. Is a free book and really good read

### Computer Networks: A Systems Approach
How the world stack of networking technology is designed around us and a lot to think of how each layer communicates is a free book and really good read.
